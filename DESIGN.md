## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-fashion, editorial visual presence for an artisanal floral atelier rooted in Mexican heritage and contemporary luxury. The identity balances regional warmth with couture refinement, catering to discerning clientele commissioning bespoke floral art, elaborate event styling, and signature gift arrangements.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactility**:
- **Dramatic Typography:** Expressive, high-contrast serif headlines evoke the world of high-fashion monographs and bespoke stationery, framed by disciplined modern sans-serif body typography.
- **Architectural Crispness:** Soft, restrained corner radii (4px) preserve structured, clean silhouettes reminiscent of premium gift boxes, linen ribbons, and bespoke botanical packaging.
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Velvety burgundy tones paired with muted sage greens and warm linen ivories recreate the intimate ambiance of an upscale floral studio.
- **Tone of Voice:** Sophisticated, poetic, and confident, emphasizing craftsmanship, botanical rarity, and the emotional power of artisanal arrangements.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural floral pigments and high-end botanical materials:

- **Primary (`#83283E`):** A deep, saturated velvet burgundy serving as the core anchor. Used for primary calls-to-action, prominent brand identifiers, luxury focal points, and key interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#4A6741`):** A foliage-inspired botanical sage green providing organic grounding. Utilized for organic tags, botanical classification chips, secondary accents, and freshness indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`):** An understated champagne gold used sparingly for micro-details, premium tier badges, VIP indicators, and delicate decorative dividers.
- **Neutral (`#242120`):** An espresso charcoal supplying authoritative editorial contrast without the stark harshness of pure black.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm ivory (`#FAF7F2`) providing a textured linen warmth.
- **Surface Layer:** Warm cream (`#F3EDE2`) for interactive cards, sheets, and elevated modules.
- **Surface Subdued:** Delicate blush tint (`#FDEEF2`) for soft highlights, seasonal promos, and hover transitions.
- **Borders & Rules:** Subtle champagne sand (`#E6DCD1`) maintaining spatial clarity through quiet definition.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast luxury of **Bodoni Moda** with the structural precision and legibility of **Manrope**:

- **Display & Headline Levels (Bodoni Moda):** Command editorial prominence. Letter-spacing is subtly tightened for headlines to heighten editorial tension, mimicking printed art catalogs and bespoke stationery.
- **Body & Functional Text (Manrope):** Provides optical comfort for descriptions of flower varieties, care instructions, and e-commerce flows.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Manrope All-Caps):** Key metadata tags, category indicators, and status chips leverage uppercase styling with positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to preserve crisp structure.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous breathing room to evoke an upscale atelier:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas of 1360px, 24px gutters, and 48px outer margins. Asymmetrical and editorial arrangements (e.g., 5-column floral showcase adjacent to 7-column narrative panels) are favored over generic symmetric grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Product catalogs compress gracefully into two-column arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 20px canvas margins. Vertical rhythm is preserved through generous cross-section padding (minimum 40px) to prevent crowded presentation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy favors planar surface layering, delicate borders, and warm, diffused ambient shadows rather than stark digital drop shadows:

- **Flat/Resting (`Elevation 0`):** Base canvas (`#FAF7F2`) with cards sitting on `#F3EDE2` defined by hairline borders (`1px solid #E6DCD1`).
- **Subtle Lift (`Elevation 1`):** Applied to interactive cards on hover, bespoke order drawers, and sticky filter headers:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(36, 33, 32, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(131, 40, 62, 0.03);`
- **Floating Overlays (`Elevation 2`):** Reserved for floral composition modals, order customization drawers, and luxury cart preview overlays:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(36, 33, 32, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(131, 40, 62, 0.04);`
- **Tonal Layers:** Backdrop scrims leverage warm charcoal tones with subtle opacity (`rgba(36, 33, 32, 0.45)`) combined with a gentle backdrop blur (`4px`) to preserve warm focus on the foreground sheet.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (Level 1)** geometry, establishing a disciplined 4px (`0.25rem`) standard corner radius:

- **Primary Geometry:** 4px radius across buttons, standard inputs, cards, image frames, and promotional banners. This maintains the clean, architectural silhouette of luxury presentation packaging.
- **Elevated Modals & Large Panels:** 8px radius (`rounded-lg`) on expansive dialogs and slide-over panels for gentle containment.
- **Exceptions:** Botanical status pills and badge counters maintain full circular ends (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectilinear card compositions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid burgundy (`#83283E`) background, crisp warm ivory (`#FAF7F2`) text, 4px border radius. Padding: 12px vertical, 24px horizontal. Hover state transitions to a deepened wine tone (`#6B2032`) with a micro-lift.
- **Secondary (Outline):** Hairline border (`1px solid #83283E`), transparent background, `#83283E` text. Hover fills with delicate blush (`#FDEEF2`).
- **Tertiary (Artisanal Botanical):** Solid sage olive (`#4A6741`) with ivory text, used for botanical customizers and eco-packaging selections.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Understated text with an animated bottom border; uses `Bodoni Moda` italicized labels for poetic CTAs (e.g., *“Explore Bouquet Collection”*).

### Cards (Floral Showcase & Collections)
- **Base Style:** Background in linen cream (`#F3EDE2`), bound by a 1px border in `#E6DCD1`, 4px radius.
- **Image Container:** Aspect ratio 4:5 or 1:1, overflowing to top edges, framed by a soft interior inset or borderless bleed.
- **Metadata Section:** Padding of 20px. Features category tags in `label-sm` sage olive (`#4A6741`), titles in `Bodoni Moda` (`title-lg`), and price tags rendered with editorial weight.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default:** Warm ivory background, 1px border in `#E6DCD1`, charcoal text (`#242120`), 4px radius.
- **Active / Selected:** Velvet burgundy fill (`#83283E`), warm ivory text, borderless.
- **Botanical / Material Pills:** Soft sage tint background (`#EEF3EC`), dark olive text (`#2E4527`), fully rounded pill shape.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Form Controls:** 44px minimum touch height, background `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #E6DCD1`, 4px corner radius.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#83283E` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #83283E`).
- **Typography:** Placeholder text styled in subdued charcoal (`rgba(36, 33, 32, 0.45)`), value text in bold `#242120`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square with 2px radius. Inactive border in `#E6DCD1`; active state filled in `#83283E` with a custom warm ivory checkmark.
- **Radios:** 18px circle. Inactive border in `#E6DCD1`; active state reveals an inner concentric dot in `#83283E`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Card Dedicatoria (Gift Message Inset):** Styled as a luxurious deckle-edge correspondence card using `#FAF7F2` with subtle champagne gold borders (`#D4AF37`) and an italicized serif script placeholder for personalized messages.
- **Stem & Ribbon Selector:** A visual swatch grid pairing thumbnail previews of ribbon textures (seda, terciopelo, yute) with subtle radio-badge overlays.
- **Freshness & Delivery Badge:** A micro-badge incorporating secondary sage green (`#4A6741`) with an artisan leaf glyph, signaling guaranteed studio delivery and floral longevity.
